**As a** scheduler, **I want to** express any of the five supported constraints in
plain English and have the re-solve honor them, **so that** I can shape the roster
(more volume, day locks, exclusions, hour caps) without editing solver code.

bounds (VAL-01), riding on the partial-apply contract from Plan 02-01.

parsed, validated, applied, and honored by the real CP-SAT engine.
