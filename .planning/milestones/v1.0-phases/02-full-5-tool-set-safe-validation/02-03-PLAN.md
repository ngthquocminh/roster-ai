<objective>
Detect degenerate re-solves and flag them honestly. When a task family with real
demand collapses to zero assigned supply, surface a structured warning in the run
result rather than narrating the solve as a success.

## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** scheduler, **I want to** be warned when a re-solve leaves a demanded task
family with no coverage at all, **so that** I am not misled into thinking a
degenerate schedule succeeded.

Purpose: Delivers ENG-05 / criterion 4 — degeneracy detection-and-flag.
Output: A warnings surface on SolveResult that flows through serialization into
runs.result_json for a future insight step (Phase 3) to narrate.
</objective>
